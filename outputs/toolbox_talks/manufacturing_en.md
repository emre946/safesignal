# 5-minute toolbox talk: Manufacturing

## Why this matters today
In 7,340 severe injury reports from Manufacturing, the most common causes were:

- **Caught in / between machinery** - 46%
- **Struck by objects / equipment** - 18%
- **Slips, trips and same-level falls** - 10%

44% of these reports involved an amputation.

## What really happened (real OSHA reports)
> *Caught in / between machinery* (2015-12): An employee was clearing a wrap-up when her hand got caught between two rollers as she was re-threading the web. She partially amputated three left-hand fingers.

> *Struck by objects / equipment* (2015-01): An employee was trying to pull down a guard on a miter saw when he had his left thumb amputated.

## Warning signs - report these as near-misses
- **Caught in / between machinery:** Guards removed, bypassed or loose; Clearing jams or cleaning while the machine is energised; Gloves, sleeves or rags near rotating parts
- **Struck by objects / equipment:** Loads lifted over people or without tag lines; Unsecured material stacked at height; Tools or parts dropped from above (even with no one hit)
- **Slips, trips and same-level falls:** Oil, water or debris on walkways; Poor lighting or cables across the floor; Rushing while carrying loads

## What we do today
- [ ] Lock out / tag out before clearing, cleaning or maintenance - every time
- [ ] Never reach past a guard; report missing guards immediately
- [ ] Stay out of the load path; use tag lines
- [ ] Secure tools and material at height
- [ ] Clean spills at once and keep walkways clear
- [ ] Wear slip-resistant footwear

## Questions for the crew
1. Where on our site could this happen this week?
2. Has anyone seen one of these warning signs recently?
3. What will we do differently on this shift?
